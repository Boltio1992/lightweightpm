Mockup note: the legacy workbook held one PM Status, one Technical Status and one Technical Percent per row, so a record could read Completed in one column and Not Started in another. This redesign stores six independent dimensions and never derives one from another without an explicit, auditable rule. The eight KPI values, the lifecycle and type counts and the unassigned-work count on this screen are all calculated from the twelve synthetic register records; the remaining service-level figures are illustrative examples drawn from the same dataset.

    Intake guards

    Triggered as the record moves
    MON-006 — UAT transition guard
    Moving a delivery item to UAT without a UAT owner and planned dates is blocked and the missing fields are displayed. That guard is enforced server-side, not just in this form.
    MON-007 — closure control
    Marking delivery complete while tasks are still open is blocked, or requires a documented exception approval (scenario A-05).
    Duplicate demand check
    Similar titles and the same application raise an advisory link to the existing item — advisory only, never an automatic rejection.

    Operational Follow-up

    Ad hoc and support work that may never become a project, but is tracked with a real owner, a real next action and a real follow-up date. Rows past their follow-up date are surfaced automatically.

    Scenario A-06 2 overdue

    Overdue follow-ups appear here and notify the support owner
    Where the follow-up date has passed and no outcome has been recorded, the item is listed in the overdue view and an escalation is raised. Narrative updates never replace the structured owner, date and next-action fields.
    ItemTopicSupport ownerNext action Follow-up dateTimelineStatus dimensions
    Lifecycle  Operational items use the same six dimensions as every other work item. Task Status tracks the individual action, not the item as a whole.

    Escalation path for an overdue follow-up

    Day 0 — follow-up date passes
    No outcome recorded. The item moves into the overdue follow-up view and the support owner receives a notification.
    Day 3 — reminder
    Reminder issued to the support owner. Threshold is configurable per priority.
    Day 7 — escalation
    Escalated to Sample Owner A with the dependency and blocker details attached. Escalation level and timing are configuration.

    Promotion paths

    An operational item never has to become a project
    May stay independent
    OPS-001 remains an operational item with its own owner, follow-up date and history. It is not forced into the delivery backlog.
    May be linked later
    It can link to a request, delivery item or task without changing type, preserving its own history.
    May be promoted
    Promotion creates a new work item of another type and records the lineage back to OPS-001, exactly as a study conversion records a link to its decision.

    Record decision

    REQ-2026-0001 · A decision record is separate from a status transition. Both are written, and both are auditable.

    The rationale is mandatory: a decision without a stated reason cannot be reviewed.
    Both records are written. The decision stores actor, action and rationale; the status history stores the previous stage, the new stage, the timestamp and the actor. Appendix A scenario A-07 requires exactly this: decision, reason, actor, timestamp and previous stage all visible on the record.
